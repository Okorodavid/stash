Stash Exit

Withdraw from your Stash vault even if the Stash app and servers are gone. This page has no backend: it reads and writes the blockchain directly through a public RPC and your own wallet.

Use the wallet that owns the vault: your exported Stash recovery key imported into a browser wallet. This page never asks you to paste a private key.
